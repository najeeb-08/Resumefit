ResumeFitv2

Match your resume to one job

Upload or paste a resume and a job description. You get a job match score, an ATS score, and suggestions written for that job. Scores update as you edit.

Example data is loaded. Replace it with your own and the scores update.

Rank many jobs against your resume

Upload your resume and a CSV of job listings. Each row needs a description column. Title, company and url columns are optional. Up to 200 rows. Then use the skill gap planner to see which skills to learn first.

Example data is loaded. Replace it with your own resume and CSV.

Rank many resumes against one job

Drop a pile of resume files (PDF, DOCX or TXT), upload a CSV with one resume per row, or both. Use the ranking to decide what to read first, and keep a person in the decision.

Example data is loaded. Replace it with your own job description and resumes.
Resumes: files and/or CSV

Drop several files at once. Each PDF, DOCX or TXT is one candidate.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing you paste or upload is sent anywhere. The first PDF you open loads the open-source pdf.js reader from a public CDN; the file itself stays on your device. Scores are estimates from keyword and rule checks. Real ATS systems differ, and a ranking is a starting point for a human decision.
